# 💚 Code of Conduct

NOAPI? is a small, friendly project, and we want it to stay that way. This page explains what that means in practice.

## 🤝 Our promise

Everyone should be able to take part without being harassed or put down, whatever their age, background, identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, beliefs, or the tools they use. Newcomers are especially welcome. Everybody started somewhere.

## ✅ What we like to see

- Being patient and kind, especially with people who are new
- Assuming good intentions, and asking before assuming the worst
- Giving feedback about the code or the idea, not about the person
- Saying thank you, and giving credit where it is due
- Admitting mistakes and learning from them
- Staying on topic, so issues and pull requests stay useful for everyone

## 🚫 What is not okay

- Insults, mocking, personal attacks or trolling
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private
- Sexual language or attention that is not wanted
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or personal data, without their permission
- Pressuring people to work faster or demanding answers from volunteers
- Anything else a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a friendly project space

## 🌍 Where this applies

In this repository, meaning issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere else you are clearly speaking for the project.

## 🛠️ What happens when someone crosses the line

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock or close a conversation, and block people who repeatedly or seriously break these rules. A friendly reminder usually comes first, but serious cases may skip straight to removal.

## 📮 Reporting a problem

If you see or experience behavior that breaks these rules, please report it privately. Contact the maintainer through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled with discretion, and the person who reports is not exposed.

If the problem involves the maintainer, you can also contact GitHub through their support channels.

## 🙏 Thanks

This page is written in plain language on purpose. It is based on common sense and the same spirit as many open source communities, including the ideas behind the Contributor Covenant. Be good to each other and have fun building things.
